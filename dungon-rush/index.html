Dungon Rush Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026 · Android package: org.dungonrush.game

This policy explains how Dungon Rush handles information. Contact the developer at sdujack2012@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Offline gameplay and local saves

Dungon Rush is an offline, single-player game. It does not require an account, run a player server, or send your gameplay, progress, settings or device identifiers to the developer. Progress, equipped items, achievements and settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. The current Android release does not request internet access or enable Android app-data backup.

No advertising or tracking

The game contains no advertising, advertising identifiers, third-party analytics, social chat, in-app purchases or player-account system. We do not sell player information or collect personal information through gameplay.

Device features

The game can use the vibration permission for optional haptic feedback. You can turn vibration off in the game’s settings. The game does not request access to your contacts, precise location, microphone, camera or shared photo library.

Support messages

If you choose to email the developer, your email address and the information you include are used to respond to your enquiry and resolve the issue. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the enquiry and related obligations. You may request deletion of your correspondence by contacting the support address above.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app’s storage through Android settings or uninstall the game. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes saved progress. There is no online game account to delete and no developer-held copy of your saved game. Device security and access to local data are managed by Android.

Google Play and external services

Google Play distributes the game and its install-time assets. Google and your device platform may process installation, platform diagnostics or other information under their own policies. That platform processing is separate from the game’s offline save system. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose hosting service may process routine web request information when you visit it. Opening an external page uses your browser.

Age group

Dungon Rush is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. The game does not collect personal information from players through gameplay.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the game’s functionality or information handling changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. For questions or requests, email sdujack2012@gmail.com.
