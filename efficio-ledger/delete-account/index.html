Efficio Ledger Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of an Efficio Ledger account and its associated data, including if you can no longer access the app.

Delete your account in the app

If you can sign in, open Settings, choose Delete my account, and follow the confirmation steps. This deletes the account and associated Efficio Ledger data.

Request deletion without app access

Email support@efficioledger.io from the email address associated with your account. Use the subject “Efficio Ledger account deletion” and ask us to delete your account and associated data. If you cannot email from that address, contact support and we will explain how to verify account ownership. Please do not include receipts, passwords, or other sensitive information in your request.

Data deleted

When we process a verified request, we delete the Efficio Ledger account and associated app data, including profile details, expense and recurring-expense records, imports, budgets, usage records, and files uploaded with receipts or imports.

Retention

We do not keep the deleted account data in Efficio Ledger after processing the request. We may retain limited information where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes, only for as long as that requirement applies. Payment providers may retain their own transaction records under their separate policies.

Deleting your Efficio Ledger account does not itself erase records held by a payment provider. For questions about a subscription or payment record, contact support@efficioledger.io.
