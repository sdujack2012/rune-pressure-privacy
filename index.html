Rune Pressure 3D — Privacy Policy
Effective 22 October 2026
Contact: sdujack2012@gmail.com

Rune Pressure 3D is an offline, single-player game. It does not require an account, display advertisements, offer in-app purchases, or use analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. The game does not collect or transmit personal data.

Data stored on your device
The game saves your best score and haptic feedback preference locally in its app storage. This data is used only to remember your settings and record. You can remove it by clearing the game's app storage or uninstalling the game. Your device or operating system may independently back up app data according to your device settings.

Permissions and sharing
The game uses optional vibration for feedback. Its Android build does not request internet access. We do not sell or share gameplay data. Google Play and your operating system process data independently under their own privacy policies.

Support email
If you contact sdujack2012@gmail.com, the information you voluntarily send, including your email address, is used to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive information. You can request deletion of support correspondence using the same address, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Audience
The game is intended for players aged 13 and above. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes
If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect. Contact sdujack2012@gmail.com with privacy questions.
